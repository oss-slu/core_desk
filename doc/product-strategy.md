# Product Strategy

**BLUF**

This project management tool is being developed to support Saint Louis University’s Core Facilities, including SLU CAM, the Chemistry Core lab, and the Academic Tech Commons (ATC). These facilities handle complex research, fabrication, and academic support workflows, yet their current project management options impose administrative burdens, limit configurability, or fail to fit their academic environment.

Our tool enables Core Facilities to focus on their work rather than the project lifecycle, providing streamlined tracking, billing ledger functionality, and upcoming scheduling capabilities. The strategy below outlines the vision, roadmap, and technical direction that will guide ongoing development.

## Product Vision

### Vision Statement

To provide a streamlined, customizable project management system that empowers Core Facilities to track work, communicate with users, and manage equipment and personnel time with minimal administrative overhead.

## Mission

Deliver a stable, intuitive platform that serves both novice users and power administrators, supports academic workflows, and scales to additional university Core Facilities.

Success Criteria
- Increased weekly active use in SLU CAM, Chemistry, and ATC
- Reduced project lifecycle friction for facility staff
- Ability to onboard new Core Facilities without custom engineering
- User-reported improvements in ease of use and workflow visibility
- Sustained contributor activity from SLU OSS development team

## User Research & Needs

Target Users
- Admins (Power Users): Core facility staff who need configurability, billing ledger visibility, permissions, and workflow control.
- General Users: Students and faculty submitting work requests who require simplicity, clarity, and fast task completion.
- Contributors: SLU OSS developers responsible for maintaining and enhancing the tool.

Observed Pain Points
- Confusion navigating project lifecycle states
- Billing handled manually in spreadsheets (or even worse, manual paper notes)
- Existing commercial tools (e.g., GrabCAD Shop, TeamDynamix) are too heavy, rigid, or costly
- Novice users need clearer UI/UX
- Admins need more customization and configuration options
- Scheduling of equipment/person time is currently outside the system and uncoordinated

### Discovery Insights

Feedback from in-person conversations, Slack discussions, and the in-app feedback form consistently highlight:
- Desire for simpler novice workflows
- Desire for deeper admin-level configurability
- Need for integrated scheduling to unify projects and equipment usage

## Market & Competitive Analysis

Direct Alternatives
- GrabCAD Shop: Strong for fabrication labs but expensive, rigid, and oriented toward industry rather than Core Facilities.
- TeamDynamix: Enterprise-level ticketing system, overly heavy for small academic cores.

Differentiation

Our product:
- Is lightweight and academically aligned
- Offers customizability without enterprise overhead
- Has no licensing cost barriers for university adoption
- Can evolve quickly based on SLU OSS input and user feedback
- Provides features tightly mapped to actual Core Facility workflows

## Positioning

A focused, stable, academically aligned project management platform purpose-built for Core Facilities seeking:
- Ease of use for students and faculty
- Configurable workflows for staff
- Tight integration of project lifecycle, billing ledger, and equipment scheduling

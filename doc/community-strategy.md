# Community Strategy

**BLUF**

Our community strategy focuses on supporting two groups: Core Facility users and SLU OSS contributors. With ~170 total users, ~30 weekly active users, and four active developers, the community is small but growing. The strategy aims to strengthen user feedback loops, support contributors through improved tooling and docs, and create a sustainable environment where both groups can shape the tool’s future.

## Community Vision

### Vision Statement

To cultivate a collaborative, academically aligned community where Core Facility staff, students, faculty, and contributors work together to improve a shared project management platform.

Goals
- Provide easy, responsive feedback channels for users
- Support an approachable, well-documented contributor experience
- Ensure the project can outlive any single team member
- Maintain alignment between product direction and community needs
- Balance configurability for admins with simplicity for novices

Success Metrics
- Increase in meaningful feedback submissions
- Healthy contributor activity (PRs, discussions, onboarding)
- Reduction in duplicated features or confusion-driven support requests
- Sustained weekly active use
- Clear governance process used consistently

### Target Community Members

Users
- Admins (Power users): Core Facility staff needing advanced config, billing, and workflow control.
- General Users: Students/faculty needing fast, simple submission and tracking.

Contributors
- SLU OSS developers currently maintaining the system
- Potential new SLU OSS fellows or campus-affiliated developers
- Future research assistants or technical staff at adopting facilities

## Community Engagement Tactics

- In-app feedback form
- Sentry user feedback
- Zoom conversations with facilities
- Occasional in-person “what do you use for project management?” conversations
